How to read the grades

These are No Brand reads on the 20–80 scale, not model grades. There is no pitch tracking from the 2026 Georgia season in hand, so each pitch is graded from the shapes we measured in our own in-season and bullpen work, from the season's results, and from reported velocity and spin. Present is the pitch as it played in 2026. Future is what the winter plan is aiming at, and it is only credited when tagged pen data gets there.

Why 45. The velocity is real and the pitch still got outs up in the zone, which is the only reason it is not lower. But in 2026 it was a cutter without the intent: the ride and the arm-side run from 2025 were gone, the release dropped on every pitch, and three in-season grip fixes did not bring it back. A fastball he could not trust for shape is also the walk problem: 46 walks, about 11% of batters.

What moves itPath A in the pen plan: 9"+ IVB with arm-side run back, with the slot cue, on 2 of 3 throws across Pens 3 and 4. If the ride comes back at this velocity it is a 55 or better. If it does not, this pitch stops being graded as a four-seam and becomes the cutter below on purpose.

Cutter

Present50
Future55
ReportedBlends with the fastball

Why 50. The glove-side workhorse in the Texas plan: off the barrel away from righties, in on the hands of lefties, and the pitch he stole strikes with on the outer edge. The honest caveat is that in 2026 the four-seam and the cutter were one family with two speeds. The one that beat him was the cutter that backed up over the middle, the only pitch a hitter could extend on.

What moves itPath B turns the accident into a pitch: the harder, fingertip cutter hold with fastball intent, graded in the band IVB 8–12", HB −2 to −6", 2–4 mph off the old fastball. A cutter that keeps ride at that speed is a 55. Most failed cutters turn into slow sliders, which is what the band guards against.

Why 60. His highest-spin pitch and the primary swing-and-miss pitch all season. Down and away to righties for the chase, backfoot to lefties when ahead. The 15-strikeout complete game against Texas at the College World Series ran through this pitch and the curve, and it breaks toward first base like everything else he throws, so it pairs off the cutter line without any extra work.

What moves itNothing, on purpose. The winter job is maintenance: hold IVB and HB within 2" of the lab-day baseline while the fastball and splitter work happens. The grade only changes if the slot cue moves it, which the pens will show.

Why 55. The depth pitch. Down and away to righties for the chase, and the second half of the two plus breaking balls that carried the strikeout rate. It is a half grade under the slider because it is a slower version of the same glove-side direction: a third pitch that breaks toward first base adds depth, not a new look for a lefty.

What moves itSame as the slider: hold shape within 2" of baseline. Its value goes up on its own once something runs the other way, because the hitter can no longer sit on one direction.

Why 40. It is not a 2026 pitch; he did not throw it to a hitter. The grade is for a shape that showed up once, in a test, and has not been repeated or located. The shape itself is the one we want: it is the first thing in his arsenal that moves away from a left-handed hitter, which is the structural hole in the whole profile.

What moves itGate B in the pen plan: 60% of splitters in the band across Pens 3 to 8 and 50% on the spot in Pens 7 and 8, then lefty swings and misses in the live at-bats of Pen 12. That is a 55. The full project is on the Pitch Design page.

  • Strikeouts came from the breaking balls. 11.2 per nine with a fastball that had lost its shape says the slider and curve are carrying more than their share.
  • Walks are the fastball tax. 4.3 per nine, about 11% of batters. A fastball he cannot trust for shape gets nibbled with.
  • The lefty hole is structural, not command. Every pitch breaks toward first base. The Texas plan had to be built around that, and it is the reason the splitter and sinker exist as projects.
  • The year got better while the fastball got worse. 6.01 to 3.68 with the four-seam drifting the whole time. The breaking balls and the competitiveness did that, which is the reason the organization paid for him in the third round.
