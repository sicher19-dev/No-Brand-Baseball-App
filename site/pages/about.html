How we workReel 03

We watch the same film the hitters do.

You pitch for a living. Every start ends up on film, and every lineup you face has people studying it. They look for the pitch you lean on, the spot you miss to and the small thing your glove does before a changeup.

You should know all of it before they do. We watch the same film and we measure it. Then we tell you what we found, in plain words, and what to do about it.

What that means

  • Every report is built for one pitcher
  • Plain words first, numbers second
  • You message the people who did the work
