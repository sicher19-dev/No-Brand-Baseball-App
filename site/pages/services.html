A season on film.

The work follows the calendar you live on. There is something for the day before a start, the day after and the months in between.

Before the start

01

Walk out with a plan for every hitter.

Before you pitch, we study the lineup you are about to face and write it up for you. You get one page for each hitter, with where to attack him and where to stay away.

You get

  • A pregame scouting report
  • A short plan for each hitter
  • Where to attack and where to stay away
  • All of it on your phone before you throw

After the start

02

See what you threw, where it went and what it did.

After each outing we go back through every pitch. The reverse report shows what you threw and how hitters handled it. The grades show how each pitch is moving from start to start.

You get

  • A reverse report after each outing
  • A grade for every pitch you throw
  • How often you hit your spot
  • Zone usage, pitch by pitch

All winter

03

Use the offseason on purpose.

The offseason is when a pitch gets sharper or a new one gets built. We set the plan for your arsenal, measure your delivery joint by joint and give every bullpen a purpose.

You get

  • Pitch design, to sharpen a pitch or build one
  • Motion-capture delivery analysis
  • An offseason arsenal plan
  • A purpose for every bullpen

Any time of year

04

Find the tell before a hitter does.

Tip Detection checks video of a pitcher for anything in his delivery that gives the pitch away before release. If something is there, the report shows what it is, when it shows up and how much it gives away.

It is built for teams and college programs as well as individual pitchers.
