## Description (4000)
No Brand Baseball is the private app for pitchers who work with No Brand Baseball Analytics.

Sign in and your page is waiting: today's scouting report at the top, then every reverse report, pitch-grade breakdown, execution report and lab we have built for you, all interactive and updated the moment we publish.

Remote assessment: record a short mobility video right in the app and send it to your coaches. You will see when it has been received and reviewed, with their notes.

Messages: a direct thread with the No Brand coaching staff, inside the app.

Built for the arms we work with in the big leagues and the minors. An account from No Brand Baseball Analytics is required.

## App Review notes
This app is for No Brand Baseball Analytics clients only; every user is a professional pitcher or coach we work with, so there is no public sign-up.
Review login (pitcher): username Demo, password Pitcher.
The demo account has sample reports, can record and upload an assessment video, and can send a message to the coaches.
Reports open inside the app; they are our own interactive analytics pages.
Camera and microphone are used only for the optional assessment video.

## App privacy (questionnaire answers)
Data collected, linked to the user, used for App Functionality only, not for tracking:
- Contact info: Name (pitchers), Email address (coaches)
- User content: Photos or Videos (assessment videos), Other user content (messages, notes)
- Identifiers: User ID (internal account id)
Not collected: location, contacts, health data, purchases, browsing, usage data, diagnostics, advertising data.
