    The ideaReel 01

    You pitch for a living. Every start ends up on film, and every lineup you face has people studying it. They look for the pitch you lean on, the spot you miss to and the small thing your glove does before a changeup. You should know all of it before they do. We watch the same film and we measure it. Then we tell you what we found, in plain words, and what to do about it.

    Tip DetectionReel 03

    Find the tell before a hitter does.

    Tip Detection checks video of a pitcher for anything in his delivery that gives the pitch away before release. If something is there, the report shows what it is, when it shows up and how much it gives away.

    Every camera angle and delivery gets one of three verdicts

    Clear tell
    A hitter who knows what to look for could use it.
    Weak tell
    A small but real edge.
    No tell
    Nothing readable before release.

    A team sends one clip per pitch and a pitch list. We send back the report.
