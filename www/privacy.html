Privacy Policy

No Brand Baseball app · Effective November 12, 2026

No Brand Baseball is a private app for pitchers who work with No Brand Baseball Analytics ("No Brand", "we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how it is handled. The app is only for our clients and their coaches; it is not intended for the general public or for children under 13.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and, for coaches, an email address, so we can sign you in and show you your own reports.
  • Assessment videos. Short mobility videos you choose to record or upload, plus any note you attach. These exist only because you tap Send.
  • Messages. Messages you exchange with your No Brand coaches inside the app.
  • Sign-in session. A token stored on your device so you stay signed in.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos you do not choose to send, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app. There are no ads and no third-party tracking.

How we use it

Only to deliver your coaching: showing you your scouting reports, letting your coaches review your assessment videos and reply, and keeping your message thread. We do not sell or share your data with anyone outside No Brand Baseball Analytics.

Where it lives

Videos, messages and account records are stored with Supabase (hosted in the United States) in a private bucket and database that only you and No Brand coaches can access. Scouting reports are served from our website. Data is encrypted in transit.

Camera, microphone and photo library

The app asks for camera and microphone access only when you tap Record video, and photo library access only when you tap Choose from library. You can decline, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Your choices

  • Ask us to delete any video, message or your whole account at any time, and we will do it promptly.
  • Sign out whenever you like; your session token is removed from the device.
  • Revoke camera, microphone or photo access in your phone's Settings.

Retention

Videos and messages stay available to you and your coaches for the length of your work with No Brand, or until you ask us to remove them. When a pitcher leaves the program, we delete their account data on request.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: nobrandbaseball@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app. The current version always lives at this address.
