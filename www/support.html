Support

No Brand Baseball app

Signing in

Pitchers sign in with their last name as the username and first name as the password. Capitalization does not matter. If it does not work, your coach can reset it in a minute.

Coaches sign in with Coach sign in using their email address. Use Forgot password? on that screen to get a reset link.

Reports

Your page shows your latest scouting report at the top and every other report below it. New reports appear automatically; close and reopen the app if you do not see one you were told about.

Assessment videos

Tap Record video to use the camera, or Choose from library for a video you already have. Keep clips short and under the size shown on screen. Good light, full body in frame, phone propped up. You will see "Received" when it lands and "Reviewed" with a note once your coaches have looked.

Messages

The Messages section on your page goes straight to the No Brand coaches. Replies show up in the same thread.
